Welcome to St. David's

Winning Souls for Christ in Osogbo

St. David's Anglican Church stands as a beacon of faith, hope, and spiritual renewal in the historic city of Osogbo. For generations, our parish has been a vibrant spiritual home where souls are won for Christ, families are nurtured in the Word, and the love of God is shared in fellowship.

Whether you are a lifelong Anglican, seeking a new church home visiting Osogbo, or exploring the Christian faith for the first time, you are warmly welcomed at our altar.
