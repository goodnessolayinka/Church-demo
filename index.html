A Warm Welcome in His Name

Grace and peace to you in the matchless name of our Lord and Saviour Jesus Christ. Whether you are worshipping with us for the first time or have journeyed with St. David's for decades, our doors and hearts are open to embrace you in true Christian fellowship.

“Enter into His gates with thanksgiving, and into His courts with praise.” We invite you to experience the life-transforming power of God's Word and vibrant liturgy with us.
