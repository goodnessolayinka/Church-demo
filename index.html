A Heritage of Grace & Truth

St. David's Anglican Church, Osogbo stands as a historic lighthouse of the Gospel. For generations, our parish has nurtured spiritual growth, fostered deep communal bonds, and served as a spiritual home for countless believers across Osun State and beyond.

Whether you are seeking a vibrant worship community, looking to deepen your walk with God, or visiting Osogbo, our doors and hearts are wide open to welcome you into our family.
