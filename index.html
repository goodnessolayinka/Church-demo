A Beacon of Hope in Osogbo

St. David's Anglican Church stands as a spiritual home for believers in Osogbo and beyond. Under the pastoral guidance of Rev. Odelola and dedicated leadership, we are committed to preaching the uncompromised Word of God, nurturing families, and impacting our community with Christ's love.

Whether you are seeking a permanent church home or visiting Osogbo, you will find a warm, welcoming family waiting to worship with you.
