Welcome to St. David's

Winning Souls for Christ in Osogbo

St. David's Anglican Church stands as a beacon of light, spiritual growth, and unwavering devotion in Osogbo, Osun State. From generations past to the present day, our fellowship remains dedicated to spreading the Gospel and nurturing hearts in Christian love.

Whether you are seeking a church home or visiting Osogbo, our doors and hearts are wide open to welcome you into our growing family of faith.
