The Journey & History

A Rich Legacy

The Avalanche of Grace: Our Story

St. David's Anglican Church, Osogbo, was born out of an unwavering passion for the Gospel and the spiritual awakening of the Osogbo community. Like a profound spiritual avalanche, the early seed of Anglican faith took deep root through the dedication of visionary founders, missionaries, and pioneer believers who sacrificed their time, resources, and energy to establish a sanctuary for generations.

From humble beginnings meeting in modest shelters to the architectural landmark of faith standing today, every milestone in our history reflects God's faithfulness and the resilience of our congregation.

Key Historical Milestones

FOUNDATION ERA

The Pioneer Mission

Early evangelists and founding fathers brought the Anglican communion to Osogbo, establishing fellowship meetings and catechism classes under challenging yet hopeful conditions.

CONSTRUCTION & GROWTH

Laying the Permanent Sanctuary

The community came together to construct the foundational church building, dedicating it to the glory of God and naming it in honor of St. David.

PASTORAL LEADERSHIP & EXPANSION

Generations of Shepherds

Guided by devoted priests and vicars — including the ongoing comprehensive documentation championed by Rev. Odelola — the church has expanded its reach, schools, and community impact across Osun State.
